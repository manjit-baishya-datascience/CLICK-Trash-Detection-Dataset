Why this matters

You can’t clean up what you can’t see.

Waste is everywhere—but reliable, diverse training data isn’t.

CLICK helps researchers build computer vision systems that work beyond the lab. Every image is captured in a real environment, carefully reviewed, and labeled using a consistent taxonomy.
